# Governance

## Maintainer

**Dipanshu Ambilkar** ([@DipanshuAmbilkar03](https://github.com/DipanshuAmbilkar03)) is the creator and sole maintainer of this project.

## Security Lead

The maintainer is also the **Security Lead**. Responsibilities:

- triage reports received through the private disclosure process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md);
- decide severity and the fix and disclosure timeline;
- coordinate releases that contain security fixes;
- keep the threat model, security policy, and dependency and CI checks current.

The Security Lead role is held by one person while the project has one maintainer. If the team grows, this section will name a security committee or a separate lead.

## Decision making

- **Routine changes** (bug fixes, tests, documentation, small improvements): the maintainer reviews and merges.
- **Significant changes** (protocol behaviour, persistence format, authentication, configuration defaults): the maintainer publishes an issue describing the change and the trade-offs, allows at least 7 days for comment, then decides.
- **Security fixes** may skip the comment period and be released as soon as a tested fix is ready. Details may be withheld until users can upgrade.

## Adding maintainers

A contributor may be invited to become a maintainer after sustained, high-quality contributions and a track record of good judgement in reviews. The current maintainer makes the final decision and updates this document.

## Conflict of interest

The maintainer should disclose any relationship that could affect a decision about the project, including employment, sponsorship, or paid work related to a competing project.

## Changes to this document

Changes are proposed by pull request and take effect when merged. The current maintainer approves changes while the project has a single maintainer.
